FAQ
How long does it take for organizations to respond?

Typically, organizations respond within 24-48 hours depending on the nature of your inquiry.

How do I enable or disable notifications?

You can manage your notification preferences in your account settings under the 'Notifications' tab.

Can I delete my account if I no longer want to use CIS Hub?

Yes, you can request account deletion by contacting support through the app or by emailing support@cis-hub.com.

Where can I submit feedback or suggestions?

You can submit feedback directly through the 'Feedback' section in the app or email us at feedback@cis-hub.com.
